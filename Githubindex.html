WEEKS 1–5 Foundations of Media Writing

Print & Broadcast News, SPJ Ethics, and Hawaiian Land Back Foundations

Traditional news media (print and radio/TV), background research, source interviews, inverted pyramid, broadcast news copyediting, and the ethics of reporting on ʻāina and community.

Weekly S.M.A.R.T. Objectives

🌿 Community Engagement Track • GenEd Ethics (E) Integrated

Aloha ʻĀina, Land Back & Ethical Communication

In ENG 359, writing is never an isolated technical exercise. Grounded in West Oʻahu, students partner with Native Hawaiian Land Back initiatives to practice respectful community protocol and ethical storytelling.

Phase 1 (Wks 1–5)

1. Listen First

No writing until after listening to Native Hawaiian community members, viewing Aloha ʻĀina, and completing an ethical reflection journal.

Deliverable: Ethics Reflection
Phase 2 (Wks 6–8)

2. Audit & Visit

In-person land visit with partner organizations. Analyze existing web and social channels to identify genuine messaging needs.

Deliverable: Recommendations Memo
Phase 3 (Wks 10–12)

3. Make & Iterate

Co-create media deliverables. Refine drafts using community feedback with human-in-the-loop oversight and accessibility checks.

Deliverable: Process Revision Log
Phase 4 (Wks 13–15)

4. Community Deliver

Present finished communication assets. The partner organization decides what gets published, how, and where.

Deliverable: Showcase Presentation

Academic Standards

Course Learning Objectives (CLOs)

Every lecture, prompting lab, and community project maps directly back to these 7 university competencies.

CLO 1 Weeks 1-5, 6-8, 9

Genre Conventions

Develop skills in genre conventions across news, PR, advertising, and business communication — with and without AI assistance.

Evidence: News article, PR package, business memo
CLO 2 Weeks 1-5, 6-8, 13-15

Research & Fact-Checking

Use and critically evaluate AI tools for research, fact-checking, and source evaluation in journalism, PR contexts, multimodal media, and business.

Evidence: Source evaluation logs, verification audits
CLO 3 Weeks 1-5, 6-8, 9, 13-15

Prompting & Editor's Eye

Write effective prompts for professional writing tasks and evaluate AI output with an editor's eye for accuracy, voice, and ethical risk.

Evidence: Prompting logbooks & rewrite comparisons
CLO 4 Weeks 1-5, 10-12

Multi-Format Media Writing

Research, interview, and write for multiple media formats, including print, broadcast, web interfaces, and social media.

Evidence: Broadcast scripts, web articles, social packages
CLO 5 Weeks 10-12

Convergence Adaptation

Adapt writing for audience, platform, and purpose in a convergence-era media environment.

Evidence: Cross-platform audience reframing
CLO 6 & 7 Weeks 9, 13-15, Final

Critical AI & Portfolio

Critically engage with AI impact on professions and articulate AI competencies clearly for a professional portfolio or job application.

Evidence: Final Capstone Portfolio & Resume Artifacts
